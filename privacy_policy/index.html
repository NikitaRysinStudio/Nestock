Privacy Policy for Nestock

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Nestock (also shown on-device as "HomeStock") is a mobile app that lets a household — for example two partners — keep a shared, synced list of household supplies and their stock levels. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is shared.

This policy applies to the Nestock mobile app on iOS and Android. It does not require a login to view and applies worldwide.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide shared household lists across your devices and the devices of the people you invite to a shared list.

DataSourceWhy we collect it
Name, email address, profile photo Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple To identify you to the people you share a household list with, and to secure your account
Item names, stock levels, item photos, comments, change history Entered by you or another member of your shared list This is the core content of the app — a shared inventory list
Push notification token Generated by your device, with your permission To notify other members of a list when an item's stock level changes
Basic technical data (e.g. crash logs, device type) Automatically, via our backend provider (Google Firebase) To keep the app reliable and to diagnose bugs

We do not collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, or any data unrelated to the household lists you and your invited members create.

2. How We Share Your Information

Nestock is built around sharing, by design, but only with the people you choose:

  • Anything you add to a shared list (item names, stock levels, photos, comments) is visible to every other member of that specific list, and to no one else.
  • Your name and profile photo are visible to the other members of any list you belong to, so they know who made a change.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Nestock has no ad network and no third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • Data is stored and processed using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging), which acts as our infrastructure provider. See Firebase's privacy and security documentation.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Removing an item or a photo deletes it from our servers.
  • Leaving a shared list removes your access to it; the list's owner can also remove members.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. We will delete your account data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

4. Security

Access to your data is restricted by server-side rules so that only the members of a given shared list can read or write its contents. Data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Children's Privacy

Nestock is intended for general audiences managing a household and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Choices

  • You can review and edit the content you've added to a list at any time within the app.
  • You can revoke push notification permission at any time in your device settings.
  • You can sign out or delete your account as described in Section 3.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
