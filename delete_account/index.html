Delete Your Nestock Account

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Nestock ("HomeStock") account and the data associated with it. Developer: Nikita Rysin Studio.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Nestock app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account ("Удалить аккаунт").
  3. Confirm the deletion. If you signed in with Apple, you will be asked to confirm with Apple once more.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email nikita.rysin.studio@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or tell us the name and email shown on your Google/Apple account). We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profile (name, email, profile photo)Deleted
Your sign-in account (Google / Apple / guest)Deleted; for Apple, the sign-in token is also revoked
Push notification tokensDeleted
Invite codes you createdDeleted
Shared lists where you are the only member (items, photos, comments, history)Deleted completely
Shared lists that have other membersYou are removed from the list and it stays with the remaining members. If you were the owner, ownership passes to another member.
Comments and change history you wrote in lists that remainKept, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user"

Backups made by our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase) may retain deleted data for a short period before being purged. See our Privacy Policy for details.
